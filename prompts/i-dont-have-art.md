# 🖌️ I Don't Have Art Yet

No assets? No problem. GB Studio ships with a default player sprite and backgrounds — your game will be fully playable. You can always swap in real art later.

For free assets, see the [free art resources](./README.md#free-art-resources) in the prompts index.

---

## Getting Started

🟢 **Just build the game, worry about art later:**
> "Create a GB Studio project called 'MyDungeon'. Set up 3 scenes: an entrance hall, a dungeon room, and a boss chamber. Use whatever default assets GB Studio has — I'll add real art later."

🟢 **Describe what you want, let the AI figure out the structure:**
> "I want to make a short Game Boy adventure. You're a kid who finds a magic door in their backyard. Two or three scenes, a friendly NPC, and a simple goal. Just build me something playable."

---

## Building Out the Game

🟡 **Add characters and dialogue without worrying about sprites:**
> "Add an old wizard NPC to the first scene. He should say 'The dungeon is dangerous. Take this torch.' Use the default sprite — I'll replace it with real art once I find a good wizard sprite."

🟡 **Scene flow and basic logic:**
> "Connect the three scenes: entrance → dungeon → boss chamber. Add a locked door between the dungeon and boss chamber that requires a key. Put the key in a chest in the dungeon."

---

## Finding and Using Free Art

🔴 **Build a game around a specific free pack:**
> "I downloaded the 0x72 dungeon tileset from itch.io. It has 16×16 sprites for a hero, merchants, monsters, and dungeon tiles. Build me a 3-scene dungeon RPG and tell me exactly which files to copy into which folders to make it work."

🔴 **Prototype first, art second:**
> "Build me the full game logic for a short ghost story — beach, lighthouse, rooftop. All dialogue, all triggers, all scene switches. Use defaults for everything. I want to get the story right before I touch any art."
